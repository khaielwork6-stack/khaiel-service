It's almost never “the algorithm hates me.” It's a leak.

Roblox recommends games based on what players do in them. Its own docs say it plainly:

“Improving your retention, engagement, and monetization directly enhances Roblox's recommendation signals.”

Roblox Creator Hub, Discovery

So when a game stalls, players are dropping out at a specific point, and that point can be found. Every game has six of them.

01The Leak Map

Six checkpoints. Find the one that's breaking.

Every Roblox game has to win the same six moments. Lose players at one, and fewer reach the next. Pick the checkpoint that hurts to see what it looks like, what I'd investigate, and exactly what fixing it involves.

02 / Hook First session

Do new players get hooked in the first few minutes?

New players decide fast. If the fun starts after a long walk, a wall of text or a menu, they're gone before they find it, and every player you gained from a better thumbnail is wasted.

  • Onboarding funnel events showing exactly which step players quit at. I'll add them if you don't have any

03 / Loop Engagement

Is there always a next thing to chase?

A game can be fun and still have short sessions. That's usually pacing: players run out of goals, or the next goal is too far away to feel real.

  • Social hooks: friends, trading, leaderboards, shared servers

06 / Spend Monetization

Do engaged players have something worth buying?

Players who love a game want to support it. When they don't spend, it's usually what's for sale, when it's offered, or how it's priced. It's rarely the players.

Roblox signals Spend days · Robux spend

Sounds like your game? Tap all that apply

What I investigate
  • When and where offers appear, compared with what players want at that moment
  • Your gamepass and developer product lineup, and its price ladder
  • The first-purchase moment: where a player becomes a payer
  • Fairness: what makes payers feel good without driving free players away
What the sprint includes

  • A pricing ladder and lineup changes
  • Shop and offer UX redesign
  • Implementation, then a readout of payer conversion, ARPPU and ARPDAU

02The deliverable

Not vibes. A ranked list of fixes.

Every audit ends the same way: your Leak Map, the evidence behind it, and a list of fixes ranked by impact and effort. You'll know exactly what to do first, whether I build it or you do.

  • Evidence, not opinions. Every finding points to a recording, a number or a comparison.
  • Ranked. Impact against effort, so the cheap, high-impact wins come first.
  • Measurable. Each fix says which number should move, and where to check it.
Sample · fictional game

Full Game Audit

Magma Factory Tycoon

Tycoon · Live 4 months · Every name and number on this page is illustrative

ClickWatch HookLeak LoopWatch D1Leak D7OK SpendWatch

Summary. The game wins clicks, then loses most new players before their first upgrade. Fix the first two minutes first. Everything after them depends on it.

  1. Evidence. Most new-player recordings quit before reaching the first dropper. Funnel step 2 (first purchase) loses more players than any other step.

    Fix. Spawn players facing their first machine. Make the first purchase possible within 15 seconds.

    Measure. Onboarding funnel step 2 completion. First play bounce rate.

    Fix with → First Session Sprint

  2. Evidence. Upgrades complete instantly. There are no offline earnings, and no goal is in progress at session end.

    Fix. Add one overnight upgrade, and show the next unlock and its timer on the leave screen.

    Measure. D1 retention by daily cohort, before vs. after.

    Fix with → Retention Sprint

  3. Evidence. Players who click expecting exploration leave the moment they see a tycoon plot.

    Fix. Test thumbnails that show the factory at full power, with the volcano as backdrop.

    Measure. Qualified play through rate, using thumbnail personalization.

    Fix with → Discovery & Launch Sprint

  4. Evidence. The offer is closed almost every time it's shown. A player at minute one can't tell what 2x cash is worth.

    Fix. Show the first offer right after the first rebirth preview, when 2x cash finally means something.

    Measure. Payer conversion rate, and time to first purchase.

    Fix with → Monetization Sprint
