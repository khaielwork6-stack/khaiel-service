    Where is your game losing players?

    Every Roblox game loses players at the same six moments. Tap the one that sounds like yours.

    In Creator Hub, check Play through rate

    People see your game, but they don't click.

    Usually the icon or thumbnail loses the click, or the game is barely being shown at all.

    What I'd do

    • Redesign your icon and thumbnails against the games you compete with
    • Rewrite your title and description around one clear promise
    • Plan a thumbnail test and a launch push for your next update
    Fix this Fix Sprint from $2,500

    In Creator Hub, check First play bounce rate

    New players leave before the fun starts.

    Usually a long walk, a wall of text or a menu stands between spawning and the first reward.

    What I'd do

    • Play it as a brand-new player on phone and PC, and record it
    • Redesign the first five minutes so the first reward lands in seconds
    • Track every onboarding step, so you can see exactly where players quit
    Fix this Fix Sprint from $2,500

    In Creator Hub, check Average session time

    Players run out of reasons to keep playing.

    Usually the next goal is too far away, or there's nothing new to chase after the first few upgrades.

    What I'd do

    • Map your core loop and how fast players progress through it
    • Rebalance rewards so a next goal is always within reach
    • Add reasons to play longer, especially with friends
    Fix this Fix Sprint from $2,500

    In Creator Hub, check D1 retention

    Players try it once and never return.

    Usually they leave with nothing unfinished and nothing waiting for them tomorrow.

    What I'd do

    • Design a reason to come back that fits your game, not just a daily reward
    • Rework how the first session ends
    • Compare Day 1 retention before and after the change
    Fix this Fix Sprint from $2,500

    In Creator Hub, check D7 retention

    Players drift away after a few days.

    Usually the game runs out of depth after day three, or nothing changes from week to week.

    What I'd do

    • Plan long-term goals like collections, mastery and rebirths
    • Build an 8-week calendar of updates and events
    • Ship the first new system or event
    Fix this Fix Sprint from $2,500

    In Creator Hub, check Payer conversion rate

    Players love it, but nobody buys anything.

    Usually the wrong things are for sale, offered at the wrong moment, at a guessed price.

    What I'd do

    • Map every offer and when players see it
    • Build a fair price ladder for passes and products
    • Redesign the shop so buying feels easy and worth it
