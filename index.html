            1. What I saw. Most new players quit before they ever reach the first dropper.

              The fix. Spawn players facing their first machine, so the first purchase happens in under 15 seconds.

              How we'll know. First play bounce rate goes down.

            2. What I saw. Upgrades finish instantly, so there's no reason to come back tomorrow.
